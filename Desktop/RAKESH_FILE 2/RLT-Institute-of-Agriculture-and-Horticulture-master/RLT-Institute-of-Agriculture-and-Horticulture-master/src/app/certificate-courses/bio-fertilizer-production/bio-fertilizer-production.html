Certificate Course in Bio Fertilizer Production

This short-term Certificate Course in Bio Fertilizer Production provides practical training in the preparation, application, and management of bio-fertilizers. Students learn about microbial culture techniques, compost enrichment, soil fertility improvement, and eco-friendly methods to promote sustainable agriculture and high-yield crop production.

Course Objectives
  • Microbial culture & bio-fertilizer production
  • Compost enrichment & soil fertility enhancement
  • Application methods for crops & sustainable practices
  • Skills for bio-fertilizer quality testing & agri-enterprises
Curriculum Highlights
  • Principles of Bio-Fertilizers & Microbial Applications
  • Microbial Culture Preparation & Mass Production
  • Carrier Materials, Formulation & Quality Control
  • Application Methods for Soil & Crop Improvement
  • Marketing, Agribusiness Skills & Practical Training

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have completed Class 10 (SSLC) from a recognized board. Interest in bio-fertilizer production, microbial applications, and sustainable agriculture is essential. Students currently appearing for their 10th exam may also apply.
