Certificate Course in Farm Management

This short-term Certificate Course in Farm Management provides practical training in farm planning, resource utilization, and modern agricultural practices. Students learn about crop and livestock management, cost-effective farming methods, farm mechanization, and sustainable approaches to ensure profitable and efficient farm operations.

Course Objectives
  • Farm planning & resource management
  • Crop & livestock management practices
  • Farm mechanization & cost-effective operations
  • Skills for agribusiness & sustainable farming
Curriculum Highlights
  • Principles of Farm Management & Planning
  • Crop & Livestock Production Management
  • Resource Utilization, Budgeting & Cost Control
  • Farm Mechanization & Sustainable Practices
  • Marketing, Agribusiness Skills & Field Training

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have completed Class 10 (SSLC) from a recognized board. Interest in farm management, crop planning, and sustainable agricultural practices is essential. Students currently appearing for their 10th exam may also apply.

Career Opportunities

Build a career with farms, agribusiness firms, agri-research centers, cooperatives, agriculture extension services, agri-tech startups, or establish your own successful farm management enterprise.
