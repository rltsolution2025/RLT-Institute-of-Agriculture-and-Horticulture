Certificate Course in Fodder & Feed Management

This short-term Certificate Course in Fodder & Feed Management provides practical training in fodder cultivation, feed formulation, and livestock nutrition. Students learn about high-yield fodder crops, silage and hay-making techniques, balanced ration preparation, soil and water management for fodder production, and sustainable practices to improve livestock productivity and farm profitability.

Course Objectives
  • Fodder crop cultivation & management practices
  • Silage, hay-making & feed preservation techniques
  • Balanced ration preparation & livestock nutrition
  • Feed management & sustainable practices
Curriculum Highlights
  • Principles of Fodder & Feed Management
  • Fodder Crop Cultivation & Preservation
  • Feed Formulation, Processing & Storage
  • Nutritional Management for Livestock
  • Agribusiness Skills, Marketing & Field Training

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have completed Class 10 (SSLC) from a recognized board. Interest in fodder cultivation, livestock feeding, and sustainable farm practices is essential. Students currently appearing for their 10th exam may also apply.
