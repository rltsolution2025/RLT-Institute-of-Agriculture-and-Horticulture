Certificate Course in Horticulture

This short-term Certificate Course in Horticulture provides practical training in crop cultivation, garden management, and sustainable horticultural practices. Students learn about fruit and vegetable production, nursery techniques, plant protection, soil and water management, and eco-friendly methods to ensure high-quality and profitable horticultural operations.

Course Objectives
  • Fruit, vegetable & flower cultivation practices
  • Nursery management & plant propagation techniques
  • Soil, water & pest management in horticulture
  • Skills for horticulture business & sustainable practices
Curriculum Highlights
  • Principles of Horticulture & Plant Science
  • Fruit, Vegetable & Floriculture Production
  • Nursery Techniques, Propagation & Greenhouse Management
  • Soil, Water & Pest Management in Horticultural Crops
  • Marketing, Agribusiness Skills & Practical Training

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have completed Class 10 (SSLC) from a recognized board. Interest in horticulture, nursery management, and sustainable crop production is essential. Students currently appearing for their 10th exam may also apply.
