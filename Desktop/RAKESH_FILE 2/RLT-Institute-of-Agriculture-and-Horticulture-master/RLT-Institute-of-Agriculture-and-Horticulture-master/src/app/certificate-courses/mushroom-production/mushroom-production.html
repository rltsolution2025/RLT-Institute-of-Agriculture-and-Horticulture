Certificate Course in Mushroom Production

This short-term Certificate Course in Mushroom Production provides hands-on training in mushroom cultivation, harvesting, and processing. Students learn about spawn preparation, substrate management, environmental control, and sustainable practices to ensure high-quality, profitable mushroom farming.

Course Objectives
  • Mushroom species selection & cultivation techniques
  • Substrate preparation, spawning & growth management
  • Harvesting, drying & storage practices
  • Skills for mushroom marketing & agribusiness ventures
Curriculum Highlights
  • Mushroom Cultivation Techniques & Species Selection
  • Substrate Preparation, Spawning & Growth Management
  • Pest, Disease & Environmental Control in Mushrooms
  • Harvesting, Drying, Packaging & Storage Practices
  • Mushroom Marketing, Enterprise Skills & Practical Training

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have completed Class 10 (SSLC) from a recognized board. Interest in mushroom cultivation, farm management, and sustainable practices is essential. Students currently appearing for their 10th exam may also apply.
