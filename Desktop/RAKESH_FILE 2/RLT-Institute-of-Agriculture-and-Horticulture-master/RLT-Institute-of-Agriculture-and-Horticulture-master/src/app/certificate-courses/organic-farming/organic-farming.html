Certificate Course in Organic Farming

This short-term Certificate Course in Organic Farming provides practical training in soil health management, natural fertilizers, and eco-friendly crop practices. Students learn about composting, bio-pesticides, crop rotation, and sustainable techniques to ensure safe, high-quality, and profitable organic farming.

Course Objectives
  • Organic crop planning & cultivation practices
  • Composting, bio-fertilizers & soil health management
  • Natural pest, weed & disease control techniques
  • Skills for organic certification & agri-enterprises
Curriculum Highlights
  • Organic Farming Principles & Crop Planning
  • Soil Health Management, Composting & Bio-Fertilizers
  • Natural Pest, Weed & Disease Control Methods
  • Organic Certification Standards & Sustainable Practices
  • Marketing, Agribusiness Skills & Field Training

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have completed Class 10 (SSLC) from a recognized board. Interest in organic farming, soil health, and sustainable agriculture practices is essential. Students currently appearing for their 10th exam may also apply.

Career Opportunities
