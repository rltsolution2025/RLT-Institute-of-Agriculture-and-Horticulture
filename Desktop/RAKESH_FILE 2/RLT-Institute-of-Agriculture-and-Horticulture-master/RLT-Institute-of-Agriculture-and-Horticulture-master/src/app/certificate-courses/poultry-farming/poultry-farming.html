Certificate Course in Poultry Farming

This short-term Certificate Course in Poultry Farming provides practical training in poultry rearing, farm management, and bird nutrition. Students learn about breed selection, housing and ventilation, feed formulation, health management, egg and meat production techniques, and sustainable practices to ensure high productivity and profitable poultry farming.

Course Objectives
  • Poultry breed selection & management practices
  • Housing, ventilation & biosecurity techniques
  • Feed formulation & bird nutrition
  • Health management & sustainable poultry practices
Curriculum Highlights
  • Principles of Poultry Farming & Breed Management
  • Poultry Housing, Ventilation & Biosecurity
  • Feed Formulation, Nutrition & Water Management
  • Health Management, Vaccination & Disease Control
  • Poultry Business Skills, Marketing & Practical Training

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have completed Class 10 (SSLC) from a recognized board. Interest in poultry rearing, poultry nutrition, and farm management practices is essential. Students currently appearing for their 10th exam may also apply.
