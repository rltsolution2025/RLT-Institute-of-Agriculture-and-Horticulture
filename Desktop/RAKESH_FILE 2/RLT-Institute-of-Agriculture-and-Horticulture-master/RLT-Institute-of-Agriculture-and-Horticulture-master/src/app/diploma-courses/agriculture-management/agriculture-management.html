Post Graduate Diploma in Agriculture Management

This intensive one-year diploma program blends modern agribusiness practices with practical agricultural training. Students will explore sustainable farming, marketing strategies, and technology-driven practices to succeed in the evolving agriculture industry.

Course Objectives
  • Master agricultural marketing & economics
  • Learn sustainable and digital farming techniques
  • Develop leadership in agri-enterprise management
Curriculum Highlights
  • Agricultural Economics & Marketing
  • Farm Management & Production Techniques
  • Post-Harvest & Agri Supply Chain
  • ICT in Agriculture & Agri-Tech Tools
  • Entrepreneurship & Project Work

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must hold a Diploma or Bachelor's degree in Agriculture, Horticulture, or Allied Sciences. Candidates with a background in Life Sciences or Rural Studies may also be considered. Final-year students awaiting results are eligible to apply.

Career Opportunities

Work with agribusiness firms, agri-tech startups, government agencies, NGOs, or launch your own sustainable agriculture venture.
