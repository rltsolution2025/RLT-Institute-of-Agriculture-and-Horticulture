Diploma in Agriculture

This one-year Diploma in Agriculture offers hands-on training in crop production, farm management, and sustainable practices. Students learn essential techniques in soil health, irrigation, pest control, and agri-enterprise for a successful farming career.

Course Objectives
  • Gain expertise in crop production and soil management
  • Learn sustainable farming and organic practices
  • Learn modern farming & agri-tech
  • Build leadership skills for agri-entrepreneurship
Curriculum Highlights
  • Crop Production & Farm Management
  • Soil Science, Irrigation & Nutrient Management
  • Plant Protection & Pest Management
  • Post-Harvest Technology & Storage
  • Agri-Business Management & Field Training

Flexible Payment Plans available on request

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have completed Class 10 (SSLC) from a recognized board. A strong interest in agriculture and related practices is essential. Students currently appearing for their 10th exam may also apply.

Career Opportunities

Build a career with agri-input companies, farming cooperatives, agri-tech startups, government agencies, or start your own sustainable agriculture-based venture.
