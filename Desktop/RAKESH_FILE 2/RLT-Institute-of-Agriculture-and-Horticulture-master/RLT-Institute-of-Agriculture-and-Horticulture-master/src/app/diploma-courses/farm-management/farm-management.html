Diploma in Farm Management

This one-year Diploma in Farm Management offers practical training in crop planning, resource management, and sustainable farming. Students gain skills in farm operations, budgeting, soil care, and agri-business for a successful farming career.

Course Objectives
  • Master crop planning & cultivation
  • Learn sustainable farm practices
  • Explore farm mechanization
  • Develop farm entrepreneurship
Curriculum Highlights
  • Crop Planning & Farm Resource Management
  • Soil Health, Water Management & Fertilizer Application
  • Pest, Weed & Disease Control Strategies
  • Harvesting Techniques & Produce Storage
  • Farm Business Management & Hands-on Training

Flexible Payment Plans available on request

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have completed Class 10 (SSLC) from a recognized board. A strong interest in agriculture and related practices is essential. Students currently appearing for their 10th exam may also apply.

Career Opportunities

Build a career with farm enterprises, agri-business firms, cooperatives, agri-tech startups, government agencies, or start your own sustainable farm venture.
