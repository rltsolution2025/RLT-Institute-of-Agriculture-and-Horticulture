Diploma in Fodder & Feed Management

This one-year Diploma in Fodder & Feed Management provides hands-on training in fodder cultivation, feed processing, and livestock nutrition. Students learn to plan and manage green fodder production, formulate balanced animal feed, and apply sustainable practices to support profitable livestock and dairy farming.

Course Objectives
  • Fodder crop planning & cultivation
  • Sustainable livestock feeding practices
  • Feed processing & storage
  • Dairy & feed enterprise skills
Curriculum Highlights
  • Fodder Crop Planning & Resource Management
  • Soil Preparation, Irrigation & Nutrient Application
  • Pest & Weed Control in Fodder Cultivation
  • Feed Harvesting, Processing & Storage
  • Livestock Feed Management & Practical Training

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have completed Class 10 (SSLC) from a recognized board. Interest in livestock care, fodder cultivation, and feed management is essential. Students currently appearing for their 10th exam may also apply.

Career Opportunities

Build a career with dairy farms, animal husbandry departments, feed processing units, cooperatives, agri-tech startups, or launch your own sustainable fodder and feed venture.
