Post Graduate Diploma in Horticultural Management

This one-year PG diploma offers hands-on training in crop production, post-harvest management, and modern horticultural techniques. Students gain expertise in fruits, vegetables, and floriculture with a focus on sustainability and agribusiness.

Course Objectives
  • Master fruit, vegetable & flower crop techniques
  • Learn organic & eco-friendly practices
  • Understand post-harvest & processing methods
  • Build leadership in horticulture business
Curriculum Highlights
  • Horticultural Crop Production & Management
  • Protected Cultivation & Nursery Techniques
  • Post-Harvest Technology & Value Addition
  • Plant Propagation & Landscape Horticulture
  • Agribusiness Management & Field Project

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have a Diploma or Bachelor’s degree in Horticulture, or Allied Sciences. Candidates from Life Sciences or Rural Studies may also apply. Final-year students awaiting results are eligible.

Career Opportunities

Build a career with horticulture-based agribusiness firms, agri-tech startups, export houses, research organizations, government departments, or start your own sustainable horticulture venture.
