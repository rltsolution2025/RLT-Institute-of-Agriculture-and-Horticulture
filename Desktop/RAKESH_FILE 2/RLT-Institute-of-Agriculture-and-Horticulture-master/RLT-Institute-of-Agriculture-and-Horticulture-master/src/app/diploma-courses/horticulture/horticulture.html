Diploma in Horticultural

This one-year Diploma in Horticultural provides practical training in fruit, vegetable, and ornamental crop cultivation. Students gain expertise in nursery management, plant protection, soil health, and eco-friendly practices for a thriving horticultural career.

Course Objectives
  • Learn fruit, vegetable & flower crop techniques
  • Learn organic horticulture & sustainable practices
  • Explore nursery management & protected cultivation
  • Build entrepreneurial skills for horticulture ventures
Curriculum Highlights
  • Horticultural Crop Production & Nursery Management
  • Soil Health, Irrigation & Fertilizer Use in Horticulture
  • Pest, Disease & Weed Management in Horticultural Crops
  • Post-Harvest Handling & Storage of Produce
  • Horti-Business Management & Practical Training

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have completed Class 10 (SSLC) from a recognized board. A strong interest in agriculture and related practices is essential. Students currently appearing for their 10th exam may also apply.

Career Opportunities

Build a career with horticulture firms, nurseries, agri-tech startups, research centers, government agencies, or launch your own sustainable horticulture-based venture.
