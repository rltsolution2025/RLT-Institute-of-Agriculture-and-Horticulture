Post Graduate Diploma in Horticultural Management

This one-year PG diploma provides practical training in plantation crop cultivation, estate management, and post-harvest techniques. Students gain expertise in tea, coffee, spices, and rubber with a focus on sustainability and agri-enterprise development.

Course Objectives
  • Gain expertise in tea, coffee, and spice crop cultivation
  • Learn sustainable practices in plantation management
  • Learn estate operations & value addition
  • Develop leadership for plantation-based agribusiness
Curriculum Highlights
  • Plantation Crop Production & Management
  • Estate Operations & Sustainable Practices
  • Post-Harvest Handling & Value Addition
  • Soil Health, Irrigation & Crop Care
  • Plantation Business Management & Field Work

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have a Diploma or Bachelor’s degree in Plantation Crops, Agriculture, Horticulture, or Allied Sciences. Candidates from Life Sciences or Rural Studies may also apply. Final-year students awaiting results are eligible.

Career Opportunities

Build a career with plantation estates, agri-export companies, agri-tech startups, research institutions, or government agencies — or launch your own sustainable plantation-based enterprise.
