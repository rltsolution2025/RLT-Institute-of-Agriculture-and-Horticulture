Diploma in Seed Management

This one-year Diploma in Seed Management offers hands-on training in quality seed production, processing, and storage. Students learn seed certification standards, genetic purity maintenance, soil preparation, and eco-friendly techniques to ensure high-yield and reliable crop performance.

Course Objectives
  • Seed production & crop selection techniques
  • Seed processing, grading & certification practices
  • Storage, packaging & quality maintenance
  • Skills for seed marketing & agribusiness ventures
Curriculum Highlights
  • Seed Production Techniques & Crop Selection
  • Pollination Control, Isolation & Genetic Purity
  • Seed Processing, Grading & Quality Testing
  • Packaging, Storage & Certification Standards
  • Seed Marketing, Enterprise & Practical Training

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have completed Class 10 (SSLC) from a recognized board. Interest in seed production, crop improvement, and sustainable agriculture is essential. Students currently appearing for their 10th exam may also apply.

Career Opportunities

Build a career with seed production units, agri-research centers, seed certification agencies, cooperatives, agri-tech startups, or launch your own certified seed enterprise.
