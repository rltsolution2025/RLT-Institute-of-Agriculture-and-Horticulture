Diploma in Vegetable Production

This one-year Diploma in Vegetable Production offers practical training in the cultivation of leafy, root, and seasonal vegetables. Students learn crop planning, soil health management, pest control, and eco-friendly practices to ensure high-quality yield and profitable vegetable farming.

Course Objectives
  • Vegetable crop planning & cultivation
  • Organic & sustainable farming techniques
  • Pest management & soil health care
  • Skills for vegetable-based agri-enterprises
Curriculum Highlights
  • Vegetable Crop Planning & Cultivation Techniques
  • Soil Preparation, Irrigation & Nutrient Management
  • Pest, Disease & Weed Control in Vegetables
  • Harvesting, Grading & Post-Harvest Handling
  • Vegetable Marketing, Enterprise & Field Training

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must have completed Class 10 (SSLC) from a recognized board. Interest in vegetable cultivation, crop care, and sustainable farming is essential. Students currently appearing for their 10th exam may also apply.

Career Opportunities

Build a career with vegetable farms, agri-marketing firms, food processing units, cooperatives, agri-tech startups, or launch your own sustainable vegetable production venture.
